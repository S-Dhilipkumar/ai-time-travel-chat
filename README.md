# 🕰️ ChronoBot – AI Time Travel Chat

Ever wondered what it would be like to talk to a **robotic nurse from Delhi, 2125** or a **street vendor in Madurai, 1700**?

**ChronoBot** lets you do just that — a generative AI chat experience powered by **Gemini API** through **Google AI Studio**, allowing users to talk to characters from the past or future.

---

## 🌟 Features

- ✨ Talk to historical or futuristic characters
- 🧠 Uses Google Gemini for personality simulation
- 🎭 Realistic and engaging roleplay based on time periods
- 🎓 Educational + Entertaining: learn while chatting!
- 📱 Minimal UI, maximum imagination

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. The user enters:
   - The **timeline year** (past or future)
   - The **location**
   - The **character type** (profession, role, etc.)

2. ChronoBot generates:
   - A detailed backstory
   - Personality traits
   - Conversational style
   - Accurate or speculative context

3. You chat — and the character responds as if they were **really there**!

---

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Google AI Studio + Gemini API** (Generative LLM)
- **HTML + JavaScript + CSS** (Frontend)
- [x] Hosted locally (can be deployed to Replit, Vercel, or Firebase)
